## R scripts for harmonizing Odonata occurrence data from German federal states

Engel, Thore, Bosch, Maya

Published 2026-08-26

Licensed mit-license



This repository contains R scripts that harmonize and merge Odonata (dragonfly and damselfly) occurrence data from the German federal states. The records were compiled for the DRAGON project, which analyses distribution trends of European Odonata. Because the German source data are particularly heterogeneous — regional databases differing in schema, spatial resolution, date conventions and taxonomic naming — they were cleaned and harmonized separately before being integrated into the DRAGON database alongside other European countries. A subset was also made available for the German national Red List of Odonata.
The occurrence data themselves are not included in this archive. Data sharing beyond the DRAGON project was not permitted by the contributing data providers, and the compiled dataset is held under restricted access. What is published here is the harmonization code, released so that comparable data mobilization efforts can build on this work rather than start from scratch.
The scripts/data_cleaning folder holds one script per federal state, each mapping that dataset onto a common target schema and documenting its particularities in inline comments; data for Berlin were obtained from GBIF rather than a regional database. merging.R combines the cleaned datasets into a single harmonized table and maps their spatial coverage, and Rote Liste export.R produces the subset used for the national Red List.
The data were compiled from regional databases with the help of the Gesellschaft deutschsprachiger Odonatologen e.V. (GdO) and the German National Research Data Infrastructure for Biodiversity (NFDI4Biodiversity); the full list of contributing providers is given in the README. This work was supported by the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) under grant agreement 442032008 (NFDI4Biodiversity), part of the National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI) programme in Germany.

## Workshop-Übung: Artvorkommensdaten strukturieren und beschreiben

Engel, Judith, Ernst, Marcus, Pauli, Matthias, Penzlin, Anke, Rach, Birgit, Weibulat, Tanja Melanie

Published 2026-05-15

Licensed cc-by-4.0



Artvorkommensdaten sind georeferenzierte Angaben dar&uuml;ber, wo und wann eine bestimmte Art (z. B. eine Pflanze, ein Tier oder ein Pilz) beobachtet oder gesammelt wurde. Diese Workshop-&Uuml;bung dient dazu, auf einige h&auml;ufige Probleme und Herausforderungen bei der Aufnahme und der Auswertung von Artvorkommensdaten aus Feldstudien aufmerksam zu machen. Sie erlaubt es den Teilnehmenden in die Rolle von Datenkurator:innen zu schl&uuml;pfen und ist als niederschwelliges Beispiel gedacht, deckt dabei aber nat&uuml;rlich nicht alle m&ouml;glichen Fehlerarten ab.
Der (Meta)datenstandard, der der &Uuml;bung zugrunde liegt, ist DarwinCore (https://dwc.tdwg.org/). DarwinCore wird prim&auml;r zur Beschreibung von Arten (species) und ihrem Vorkommen in der Natur genutzt und ist der haupts&auml;chlich verwendete Standard in GBIF (Global Biodiversity Information Facility, https://www.gbif.org/).
